# Roof and climate dataset — first review

13 selected locations; one record represents a local roof tradition or ensemble, not a sampled building. Climate values are NASA POWER regional grid estimates for 1991–2020.

## Method and limits

NASA POWER monthly T2M (°C), PRECTOTCORR (mm/day) and WS10M (m/s) were downloaded for 1991–2020. Each monthly precipitation rate was multiplied by the actual number of days in its month, annual totals summed and the 30 annual totals averaged. Temperature and wind annual means are weighted by days. Monthly climatologies average each calendar month over 30 years. Raw responses and exact API URLs are preserved. All 360 monthly values per parameter per location were checked for missing-value flags.

POWER meteorology derives from MERRA-2; its roughly 0.5° latitude by 0.625° longitude grid is much larger than a village. Mountain, valley and island estimates may differ materially from local observations; verify them against local stations before making site-specific claims. Values describe a recent baseline, not climate when these traditions originated. Mean wind does not represent gusts, cyclone risk or roof-level exposure. Precipitation includes rain and snow water equivalent; snowfall, snow load and extremes are missing. No pitch values were inferred from photographs. The Shirakawa 60° value is a general tourism-source description, not a measurement of every roof. Materials and roof forms describe traditions, not all present buildings. Selected Commons photographs, photographer credits and reuse licences are recorded in photos.json and embedded in the HTML explorer. The selected examples are purposive and geographically unbalanced; they cannot establish causation or population-wide correlations. Hahoe contains two roof materials under the same regional climate and must not be treated as two independent climate samples.
