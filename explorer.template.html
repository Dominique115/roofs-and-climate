About the data & how to read this collection

Climate sources

Precipitation includes rain and snow water equivalent. Snowfall depth is reported separately where available. The orange line shows outdoor air temperature, not indoor or roof surface temperature.

Open climate source ↗

Evidence, not assumptions

Month selections are editorial prompts, not scientific season boundaries. Explanations distinguish documented local functions from general mechanisms. Climate alone does not explain architecture; craft, materials, social life and history also matter.

Photographs show actual buildings at each location. Gallery views may show different local buildings as well as different viewpoints. Explanations discuss the documented tradition; they are not performance measurements of every pictured house. Credits link to the original file and reuse licence. Images are scaled to fit without cropping. This collection is exploratory and does not establish causation.
